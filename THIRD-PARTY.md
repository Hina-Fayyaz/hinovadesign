# Third-party assets and fonts

## Agon template

The site adapts layout patterns and uses selected photos from the Agon NuxtJS template supplied by Hina Fayyaz. The original template and bundled-asset licensing terms continue to apply. This source package is prepared for the supplied Hinova website project. No separate grant to redistribute the commercial template is made.

The current agency hero, process and Insights photography was supplied by Hina through the Google Drive folder `Hinova Design/Images` on 6 October 2026. Optimized versions are in `public/images/agency/`; original filenames and placement are documented in `docs/IMAGE-SOURCES.md`. These are illustrative photos, not representations of Hinova staff. The existing founder and testimonial portraits remain as supplied. Unused template assets, if retained, remain subject to their original licenses.

## Hinova brand assets

The five hero-strip portraits (Jeroen van Ommen, Malene Kai Bell, Milane Hughes, Patsy Cisneros and Scott Neil) were supplied by Hina for this placement. The original image files are preserved; circular framing is applied by the website styles.

The founder portrait was supplied as `hina-hero.png`. The separate personal portfolio under `public/hinafayyaz/` preserves the original page, styling, components, and supplied assets from `hinovadesign-main.zip`. Only relative URLs, canonical metadata, and form transport were adjusted for its new subpath and static hosting. Its Vimeo introduction is the one embedded in the original `Hero.html`.

The symbol and fonts were supplied in hinova-brand-presentation(2).html. The palette is taken from the supplied palette image and brand presentation. Vector logo paths preserve the supplied symbol; wordmark outlines are generated from the supplied Cal Sans and Inter font files.
